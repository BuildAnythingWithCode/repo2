Read this before you lean on it

  • It marks seasons, not days. The Very high zone first lit up —, — and — days before the last three cycle tops. On that first day in 2017, price stood at — against its eventual top.
  • Recent tops read lower. The 2021 and 2025 tops read — and —, not 1. In the blind test they read — and —. Each cycle has been tamer than the one before, and a gauge built on the past is always a little behind that.
  • Three complete cycles is three data points. A recipe that fits three cycles this well can still be fooled by the fourth. The recipe was also chosen by someone who had seen all three.
  • Today's number is soft. Nudging the design moves today's reading between — and —. CBBI, an independent public index, reads — today. Both say low. They disagree on how low.
  • It cannot see what has never happened. It measures where price sits against its own past. News, regulation and anything new to this cycle are outside its view. It is a thermometer, not a doctor.
How the number is made
  1. Collect. Free public data, re-downloaded every day: price, market value, what holders paid, mining and network activity. Sources are Coin Metrics community data, Blockchain.com, Coinbase, alternative.me and DefiLlama.
  2. Measure. The data becomes the indicators listed above. Distances from trend are divided by bitcoin's own long-run volatility, so that a doubling in 2013 and a doubling in 2025 are not treated as the same event.
  3. Give each indicator its own ruler. The level of its past lows is 0 and the level of its past highs is 1, with older history slowly fading. The ruler is read with a one-year delay, so that a blow-off cannot stretch the ruler it is being measured with.
  4. Blend. The indicators inside each lens are averaged, then the three lenses are averaged.
  5. Smooth the ends. One S-curve lets the extremes approach 0 and 1 without ever sticking to them, so the most extreme day still gets the most extreme reading.

The code, the full backtest and a plain-English write-up are in the GitHub repository BuildAnythingWithCode/repo2. Open Sources on this page to see the rows and the code behind any number.
